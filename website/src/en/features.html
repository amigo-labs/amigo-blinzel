Controls

There are no toolbars. The menu (☰) sits top left; minimize, maximize and close sit top right. Everything else is in the menu or on a key.

Image files stay untouched

amigo-blinzel never changes an image file. Cutouts are only written as new files through “Save as”. For JPEG sources the data is copied losslessly instead of re-encoded where possible.

Formats

JPEG, PNG, GIF and BMP with our own decoders; TIFF, HEIC, AVIF, WebP, JPEG XL and RAW through the Windows codecs, where installed.

Where the speed comes from

  • Decoding at screen resolution: JPEGs are decoded with DCT scaling straight into YCbCr planes; the GPU does colour conversion and scaling.
  • An own JPEG decoder spreads Huffman decoding over all cores, even without restart markers.
  • Prefetching in paging direction, with priorities and cancellation.
  • Parallel start: window, GPU and decoder start at the same time.
  • Win32 and Direct3D 11 with precompiled shaders; a single executable of about 3 MB, no runtime.

Installation and updates

The executable installs itself, for you only and without administrator rights. Updates arrive silently in the background, are checked three ways and take effect at the next start.
