Controls

There are no toolbars. The menu (☰) sits top left; minimize, maximize and close sit top right. On a Mac the system's window buttons sit there, the menu goes right and is in the menu bar as well. Everything else is in the menu or on a key; on a Mac ⌘ replaces Ctrl.

Screenshots (Windows)

In background mode the Print key takes screenshots, as with Greenshot: the picture freezes at once, you drag a region over it, a click takes the window under the pointer, Enter the whole screen. The screenshot is saved as PNG in “Pictures\Screenshots” (configurable), optionally also copied, and opens in the viewer right away. There is no editor of its own: E hands the image to the program of your choice. Ctrl+Shift+Print also works when Windows gives the Print key to the Snipping Tool. macOS and Linux follow.

Image files stay untouched

amigo-blinzel never changes an image file. Cutouts are only written as new files through “Save as”. For JPEG sources the data is copied losslessly instead of re-encoded where possible.

Formats

JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, WebP, AVIF and JPEG XL with our own decoders on every platform. HEIC and camera RAW through the installed Windows codecs on Windows, through ImageIO on macOS (HEIC with Apple's hardware decoder), and RAW through the embedded previews on Linux.

Where the speed comes from

  • Decoding at screen resolution: JPEGs are decoded with DCT scaling straight into YCbCr planes; the GPU does colour conversion and scaling.
  • An own JPEG decoder spreads Huffman decoding over all cores, even without restart markers.
  • Prefetching in paging direction, with priorities and cancellation.
  • Parallel start: window, GPU and decoder start at the same time.
  • Native on every platform: Win32 and Direct3D 11 on Windows, AppKit and Metal on macOS, Vulkan (wgpu) on Linux. No runtime, no framework in between.

Installation and updates

On Windows the executable installs itself, on Linux blinzel --install does, both for you only and without administrator rights; on macOS you drag the app into Applications. Updates arrive silently in the background, their checksums are signed with the project's release key, and they take effect at the next start. Download and installation.
